# Project Scope

## Refined title

Design and Evaluation of a Proof-of-Concept Explainable AI Copilot for Operational Business Decision Support

## Why the title was refined

The original topic was broad and included business and employee data analytics across small and large-scale organisations. The refined title keeps the same core topic but makes the implementation more manageable and evaluation-driven.

## Core implementation

The implemented proof-of-concept focuses on customer churn decision support.

## Optional / discussion-only extensions

- Employee attrition analytics
- Sales/operations analytics
- Full cloud deployment
- Unrestricted LLM chatbot
- Login system
- Enterprise production database

## Main research contribution

The strongest contribution is the evaluation of whether prediction + explanation + recommendation improves understanding, trust, usefulness and decision confidence compared with prediction-only output.
