# Evaluation Plan

## Aim

Evaluate whether Copilot-supported output improves user understanding, trust, usefulness, usability and decision confidence compared with prediction-only output.

## Conditions

| Condition | Output shown |
|---|---|
| A | Prediction-only churn risk score/category |
| B | Prediction + SHAP drivers + plain-English explanation + recommendation + safety warning |

## Suggested participants

Target 10-15 participants where possible. A smaller pilot sample can be reported honestly if recruitment is limited.

Suitable participants:

- postgraduate students
- early-career professionals
- analysts
- managers if available

## Measures

Use 1-5 Likert scale questions:

1. I understood the result clearly.
2. I trusted the system output appropriately.
3. The explanation helped me understand why the prediction was made.
4. The recommendation was useful for decision support.
5. I felt more confident making a decision after seeing the output.
6. The system made clear that human review is still required.

Also collect short comments:

- What was useful?
- What was confusing?
- Would you rely on this output? Why or why not?
