# Market and Academic Comparison

## Commercial systems are not the same as the dissertation contribution

The dissertation should acknowledge that AI-assisted analytics already exists in commercial products. The project should not claim to be a commercial replacement.

| Comparator | What it does well | Limitation for this dissertation gap | How this prototype is positioned |
|---|---|---|---|
| Power BI Copilot | AI-assisted reporting and analytics inside Microsoft BI ecosystem | Requires prepared data models, licences and enterprise environment | Local, transparent, reproducible prototype for uploaded datasets |
| Tableau Pulse / Tableau AI | Metric insight summaries and natural-language business explanations | KPI/metric centred; not focused on end-to-end XAI workflow from uploaded data | Combines cleaning, EDA, model comparison, explanation and action recommendation |
| ThoughtSpot | Natural-language governed analytics over enterprise data | Strong semantic/governance layer, but not an inspectable MSc research prototype | Shows a controlled academic version with visible logic and evaluation |
| SHAP/LIME tools | Explain individual model predictions | Explanation only; no full business workflow | Translates drivers into recommendations and safety warnings |
| TalkToModel | Conversational model explanation | Focuses on model explanation; not full upload-clean-EDA-action pipeline | Extends to operational business decision support with recommendations and evaluation |
| AutoML tools | Select and tune models | May not focus on explanation, business meaning or human evaluation | Uses controlled model comparison as part of a human-centred Copilot |

## Safe novelty statement

The project is not novel because it creates a chatbot or dashboard. Its novelty is the integrated and evaluable workflow:

```text
uploaded dataset + explainable prediction + controlled Copilot + business recommendation + safety warning + user evaluation
```
